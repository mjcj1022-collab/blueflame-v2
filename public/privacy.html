Privacy Policy

Blue Flame · Effective August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Blue Flame (“we”) collects and how we use it when you use our jewelry design service (the “Service”). By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the Service:

  • Account information — your shop name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Your content — the designs, sculpts, customers, and related records you create and choose to save.
  • Billing information — handled entirely by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store your full card details; we retain only a subscription/customer reference and status.
  • Basic technical data — standard server logs needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. How we use it

We use your information to provide and maintain the Service, authenticate you, process payments, save your work, respond to support requests, and keep the Service secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your designs to advertise to you.

3. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the Service, under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Turso — hosted database storage for your account and content.
  • Render — application hosting.
  • A metals-price provider — to fetch daily precious-metal spot prices (no personal data is sent).

4. Local storage in your browser

The app stores some data in your browser (such as your session and autosaved work in progress) so the studio works smoothly and can recover your work. This stays on your device.

5. The offline version

The offline desktop version runs on your own computer and keeps your work locally. It does not send your designs to us; cloud features are inactive in that version.

6. Data retention & your choices

We keep your account and content while your account is active. You can request a copy or deletion of your personal data, or ask us to close your account, by emailing us. We will honor such requests as required by applicable law; some records may be retained where legally required (for example, payment records).

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including hashed passwords and encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Service is intended for users 18 and older and is not directed to children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the effective date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
